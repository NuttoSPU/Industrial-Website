Trai-cor Processing, Inc. and Anthony Traicoff


Anthony Traicoff: A Leader and Steel Industry Veteran

Anthony Traicoff is a pivotal figure and a seasoned leader with over 32 years of experience in the steel industry. His expertise spans project engineering and business management, skills he has leveraged to successfully manage operations and compete effectively against global steel markets. Traicoff simultaneously holds key leadership positions, including President of Trai-cor Processing, Inc., Indiana Strip Steel & Consulting Services, LLC, and GST Industries, LLC. His professional achievements have been recognized nationally, notably through his nomination for the Ernst & Young Entrepreneur of the Year award in 2006 and other accolades such as the Businessman of the Year award.


Trai-cor Processing, Inc.: Core Business and Specialization

Trai-cor Processing, Inc. functions as an international steel supplier, consultant, and engineering firm dedicated to the Cold Strip Industry. The company specializes in the procurement and processing of High Carbon Steel and Alloy Steel—critical raw materials essential for various global industries. Trai-cor's processed materials are vital components used across major sectors, including automotive manufacturing, construction, and specialized stamping industries. By maintaining high standards of quality and leveraging deep metallurgical knowledge, Trai-cor has established itself as a reliable source for specialized steel products.
