Our Services


Consulting & Engineering Services

We provide expert metallurgical consulting to help clients select the ideal steel grades for specific applications. Our engineering team specializes in the design, installation, and refurbishment of heavy industrial machinery, ensuring your production lines operate at peak efficiency with optimized supply chain management.

Steel Processing & Fabrication

Our facilities offer high-precision slitting and cut-to-length services to meet exact dimensional requirements for various steel gauges. We utilize advanced cold rolling and temper rolling techniques to achieve superior surface quality and specific mechanical properties, ensuring every coil or sheet meets rigorous industry standards.

Strategic Steel Supply

We specialize in the procurement and distribution of high-quality hot-rolled, cold-rolled, and specialty strip steel products. By leveraging our deep industry network, we ensure a reliable flow of raw materials tailored to the demanding needs of the automotive, appliance, and manufacturing sectors.
