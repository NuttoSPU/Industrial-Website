Our Service :

We are a full service steel supply center and consulting and engineering firm for the steel strip industry. We carry a large inventory of various grades of steel including coated materials. With ability to further cold roll, anneal, temper pass and slit or cut-to-length in our Partner company, you can be assured that we got you covered. We alsospecialize in high carbon and alloy steel used in the automotive, construction and stamping industries. Also available are metallurgical services, not only in raw material design, but processing procedures for all cold rolled products.

Consulting Services :

We offer Metallurgical and Engineering Consulting Services. Metallurgical Consulting includes raw material design to achieve desired properties, processing procedures and microstructural evaluation. Our Engineering Consulting services include equipment evaluation, equipment sale, removal and refurbishing, personnel training, procedural documentation, maintenance, and management training.

Grade

We supply steel in multiple grades, including HR, HRPO, CR, CRA, Galvanized, and Galvannealed. With over 50 years of combined industry experience, we ensure our steel meets your requirements.

Chemistries

We supply carbon steel and alloy grades including 4130, 4140, and 6150, with all material tested in-house to ensure mill conformance. Material certifications and test reports are available upon request.

Products

We supply ribbon wound coils, sheets, angles, channels, round bars, flat bars, rectangular and square tubes, and pipes.

Shipping

We offer flatbeds, vans, domestic and export containers, LTL (less than truckload), and air-freight shipping options.
